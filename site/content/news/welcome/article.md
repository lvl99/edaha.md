# Welcome

**edaha.md** v0.1.0 was launched as a simple approach to a static site generator, utilising markdown to then render articles into HTML and RSS XML files.

You can find out more on our [About](/about) page, or on our [Github repo](https://github.com/lvl99/edaha.md).

**edaha.md** originally started as a simple and quick solution to have markdown files that could be easily converted to HTML for use in the [LiveCanvas](https://livecanvas.lvl99.com) project.
